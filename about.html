Purpose of the System

Why TourismHub was engineered for modern city landmarks, heritage sites, and eco-parks.

Balancing Unforgettable Visitor Experiences with Sustainable Site Capacity

Popular cultural landmarks, museums, and recreational parks often suffer from unpredictable crowd surges, long entrance queues, and delayed operational advisories. TourismHub solves these challenges with a unified digital ecosystem shared by tourists and attraction operators.

Tourists gain transparent visibility into live slot availability, destination schedules, and attraction-hosted events before traveling, while administrators maintain safe crowd thresholds, fast gate verification, and instant public announcements.

  • Prevent site overcrowding by enforcing timed entry quotas across active destinations.
  • Eliminate paper tickets and manual logs using instant QR pass generation and gate scanning.
  • Keep guests informed immediately during weather alerts, schedule changes, and special events.

System Features

Core capabilities powering tourist discovery and operator workflow management.

Attraction & Event Cataloging

Organizes physical destinations across Manila and Quezon City with dedicated linked events, cover imagery stored in Supabase Storage, operating hours, and admission details.

Capacity & Schedule Control

Interactive calendar filtering, occupancy indicators, and dynamic time-slot allocations let visitors choose uncrowded windows while helping operators cap visitor limits.

Digital QR Passes & Gate Scanner

Tourists receive user-scoped digital QR passes in their profile wallet, validated at entry gates via live camera scan, uploaded QR image, or manual code verification.

Live Advisory & Event Broadcasts

Operators dispatch weather advisories, schedule notices, and event updates that stream in real time to homepage banners, notification feeds, and filterable alert tables.

Admin Analytics & CSV Export

Role-restricted operator console provides hourly attendance curves, category utilization metrics, scan logs, and one-click CSV operational reporting.

Cloud Sync & Adaptive Dark Mode

Zero-dependency vanilla HTML/CSS/JS architecture powered by Supabase PostgreSQL real-time sync, custom avatar cropping, saved favorites, and full Dark & Light Mode themes.
